About Planet 56

Planet 56 is my next major title I plan shipping onto Steam soon. Like "Castle Siege", this is another test of my creative limits and programming abilities. One of my goals for this project is to push the limits of computer performance while still maintaining the most efficient optimization by using Unreal's performance profiling tools. While still in development, I am holding playtesting sessions much more often to ensure gameplay exceeds player expectations.
